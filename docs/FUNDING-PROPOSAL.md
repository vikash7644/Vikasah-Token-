# Vikasah Token (VKT) — Strategic Token Funding Proposal

> **Status: Draft for discussion only. Not an open token sale, investment offer, or solicitation.**
>
> No funds or tokens should be transferred on the basis of this document. Any transaction requires final legal, tax, compliance, technical, and commercial review and a separately approved agreement.

## 1. Project snapshot

- **Project:** Vikasah Token
- **Ticker:** VKT
- **Target network:** BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)
- **Planned maximum supply:** 1,000,000,000 VKT
- **Current project status:** Development stage. The token is not represented as audited, mainnet-launched, listed, or available for public purchase.

The repository's current token design describes a fixed/capped supply with no owner mint function, a 300,000,000 VKT owner-allocation wallet, and a separate 700,000,000 VKT distribution reserve. Any funding allocation must be explicitly approved, sourced from the appropriate reserve, and reflected in the public allocation records before any transfer.

## 2. Proposed funding structure

The project is exploring a **token-allocation-only** funding model. It does not offer company equity or project ownership.

| Proposed contribution | Indicative VKT allocation | Reference price |
|---:|---:|---:|
| USD 10,000 | 10,000,000 VKT | USD 0.001 / VKT |
| USD 20,000 | 20,000,000 VKT | USD 0.001 / VKT |

These are discussion figures only. They are not a confirmed valuation, guaranteed price, binding offer, or promise that VKT can be sold or exchanged. The proposed allocations equal 1% and 2% respectively of the planned maximum supply.

## 3. No equity or project ownership

Under the proposed structure, a participant would not receive:

- Shares, equity, or ownership in the project or any company.
- Ownership of source code, repositories, trademarks, or other intellectual property.
- A board seat, administrative keys, protocol control, or management authority.
- Any guaranteed return, dividend, buyback, liquidity, exchange listing, or token-price appreciation.

The only contemplated consideration is the VKT allocation described in a final written agreement, subject to applicable law and the final token terms. Token-holder rights, if any, must be stated explicitly; none should be inferred from this proposal.

## 4. Intended use of funds

Subject to a published budget and applicable approvals, proceeds may support:

1. Smart-contract engineering, testing, and deployment preparation.
2. Independent security audit and remediation.
3. Wallet, developer tooling, and ecosystem infrastructure.
4. Documentation, integrations, and community support.
5. Legal, tax, compliance, and operational costs.

The project should publish a budget and periodic use-of-funds updates. No spending or fundraising total is claimed by this proposal.

## 5. Allocation, vesting, and safeguards

Before any token allocation is accepted or distributed, the project must document:

- The exact wallet from which the allocation will be sourced.
- The participant's verified receiving address and supported network.
- Any lock-up, vesting schedule, transfer restrictions, and release dates.
- The process for handling failed transfers, refunds (if legally required), and disputes.
- Public disclosure of the allocation without exposing private keys or sensitive personal data.

No vesting schedule or investor rights are finalized by this draft. The project must not promise liquidity or imply that a lock-up guarantees future value.

## 6. Due diligence and launch gates

No token sale or distribution should begin until the project has completed and documented, as applicable:

- Review by qualified legal and tax advisers in relevant jurisdictions, including India.
- Assessment of VDA, FIU-IND/PMLA, KYC/AML, advertising, and cross-border requirements where applicable.
- Independent smart-contract security audit and remediation of material findings.
- Verification of deployment configuration, treasury/admin controls, allocation wallets, and source code.
- Final approval of the token sale agreement, disclosures, risk factors, and participant eligibility rules.

A smart contract, testnet deployment, or exchange listing does not by itself establish legal approval or create a liquid market.

## 7. Risk disclosure

VKT may never obtain a mainnet launch, exchange listing, meaningful utility, or liquid market. A participant could lose some or all of the amount contributed. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible, wallets may be compromised, and regulatory requirements may change. Participants should conduct independent due diligence and should not contribute money they cannot afford to lose.

## 8. Contact and next steps

Interested technical, ecosystem, and funding partners may first request the current development status, token contract source, test results, allocation plan, and draft legal terms. Any discussion remains non-binding until definitive documents are approved and signed by the relevant parties.
